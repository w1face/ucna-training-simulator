OFFICE OF NAVAL TRAINING · FLEET READINESS

Welcome aboard, Officer.

The United Confederation Naval Academy prepares bridge crews for the demands of active fleet service. These training assignments develop the judgement, accuracy and composure expected of every officer entrusted with a Confederation vessel.

Enter your name or callsign, select an assignment, and complete the exercise at your own pace. Your assessment record will be retained at this terminal for review. Repeated practice is encouraged; confident decisions are made long before the first hostile contact.
