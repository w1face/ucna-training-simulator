Simulator scope: The supplied BCsrcNEW.zip has a four-slider UI limit. A historical six-slider stealth ship has been reported, but is not verified from this particular source snapshot. The trainer allows up to six sliders to support training for historic or customised configurations.

Measurement rules: Every stage includes its two-second lock. The initial time is the sum of its two single-slider stages; the in-depth time is the sum of its two or three selected multi-slider stages; the complete time covers all four or five stages plus the time taken to press Scan Again between initial and in-depth. The two phase timers exclude that hand-off, so the complete time may be greater than their sum. Aborted exercises are excluded. Individual drills contribute to their relevant phase average, but only complete contacts contribute to the complete-scan average. Averages are officer-specific by default. In-depth and complete-scan averages are separated by both slider count and in-depth stage count; all averages pool scanner-power settings; each attempt records its effectiveness for comparisons. Older complete-contact results without the Scan Again hand-off remain in history but are excluded from the revised complete-scan average.
Simulator fidelity and Scanner powerRandom targets and initial positions are independently generated in the 0–100% range. Each slider starts at least 20 percentage points away from its hidden target. The combined absolute error must remain below 5 percentage points for two seconds. Waveform frequency, phase offsets, and per-point random noise follow the BCsrcNEW.zip expressions in signalQualityIndicator.cpp and scanningDialog.cpp. Below 100% effectiveness, scanners perturb the sliders; above 100%, they automatically nudge sliders towards their targets using the game's per-draw formula. The game updates these per render, so exact rates depend on frame rate; this trainer normalises the effect to approximately 60 frames per second. Its power dropdown represents effective Scanner performance, not the Engineering power request. It does not model system damage, hacking, heat, depleted reactor power or scenario-specific scanning_noise.
How to train effectivelyStart with a complete contact at 100% to establish a baseline. For in-depth stages, move one slider until the waveform is at its best, hold it, then improve the next. Return to the first only if necessary. Repeat several contacts before assessing progress. Try 150%, 200% and 230% separately to feel the difference in automatic guidance. The 230% preset represents the stated limit with 100% coolant. Performance averages combine power settings, so compare runs at the same power using the exercise history or exported CSV.
