Operating notes & simulation settings

Range and arc share a fixed beam area: increasing either can reduce the other. The radar stays at 2U; 1U = 1000 game units.

Hold draws the object towards the beam axis, just inside the selected range. Sweep too quickly and it can leave the beam. The object's heading stays unchanged.

This first sandbox uses a stationary object (radius 100), a fixed ship (radius 100), template drag of 100 units/second and full docking-system effectiveness. Hold applies twice the drag. These are practice settings, not a particular ship's specification.

No momentum, collision damage, docking, energy depletion, missiles or scoring in v0.2.3. Switching Off leaves this stationary object where it is. Reset object restores its starting position without changing your controls.
